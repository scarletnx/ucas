# Mali-G715 calibration run (third)

Measured on the Mali-G715 of a Pixel 9 (Tensor G4, Valhall, subgroups of 16 lanes, driver v1.r54p3) through SPIR-V, over adb, on an idle GPU, with five runs of each test and three tries: the same corpus as the runs before it, for how far the fitted classes move between runs.  Costs are throughput ratios to fadd in the same run; there is no SM clock to read on this device, so cycles are not given.  The geometry stage and fp64 do not run on this driver.
