# GT 1030 calibration, 2026-11-11

Driver 580.178.04 on FreeBSD; SM clock 1.2-1.8 GHz; fadd ring at 3.95 warp instructions/clk/SM (peak 4).

## Findings against the prelude gt1030 model

- Right already: iadd, logic, shift (2), imul (3: XMAD), ftrans (4), f64 (32).
- Dearer than the model: idiv 28.5 (24), idivk 8.8 (5, umod by a constant 15, sdiv by a constant 28), cmp 2.4,
  select 1.6, store 15 (8), branch 6.2 (2, a real BRA with reconvergence), diverge 14 (6).
- Cheaper: fdiv 3 (10), fsqrt 4 (8), fpow 8 (10), minmax 1 (2), bitcnt 3 (4), conv 3 (4), load 6.9 (8),
  barrier 9 (20), atomic_global 50 in L2 (64; 138 over DRAM), atomic_shared 6.6 (8).
- Fusion the per-op model misses: 2-4 logic ops in one LOP3 (lop3_select = 1 instruction for 4 ops), a*b+c
  in one XMAD chain or FFMA, (a << 3) + b, x * 2^n + b, a / 2.0001 becomes a multiply (1.3), abs and negate
  are free, the f32->f16->f32 round trip is dropped. mix is 2 (not 1), length(vec3) is 2 (not 3).
- Library calls: atan2 63 (the model says 16), sinh 8, tan 12, asin/acos/atan/tanh ~17.
- Small arms (2 FFMAs) are predicated whatever [[dont_flatten]] says; 8 and more become real branches.
- NVIDIA extras: DP4A costs 1 (emulated: 10, ucas prices the emulation at 32); MATCH.ANY
  (subgroupPartitionNV) is emulated on sm_61, 54; subgroup add 40, scan 64, shuffle 4; clock reads 6.
- Not measured: the model's f16 class (shaderFloat16 is false on GP108: no f16 arithmetic).

## Run
SM clock after the runs: 1784 MHz (nvidia-smi). Costs are throughput ratios to fadd in the same run.
Probe: 2048 warps on SMs [0, 1, 2] (gl_SMCountNV 3, gl_WarpsPerSMNV 64, highest warp slot 63).
fadd: 0.001 ns a lane-step, 3.95 warp instructions a clock an SM (sm_61 peak 4).
